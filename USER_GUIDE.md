# User guide

## First launch

A short setup wizard collects company details, logo, currency (default ZMW), tax, and document prefixes. Optional sample services can be added; no fake invoices are created.

## Typical quotation

1. Dashboard → **New quotation**
2. Select a customer
3. Add line items from the catalogue or type a custom description
4. Adjust quantity, price, discount, and tax — totals update immediately
5. Save
6. Print or **Export PDF**
7. **Convert to invoice** when accepted

## Typical invoice

1. Create an invoice or convert a quotation
2. Review totals
3. Save and send/print
4. **Record payment** (cash, bank transfer, mobile money, card, or other)
5. Partial payments show **Partially Paid** and the remaining balance
6. When the balance reaches zero the invoice is **Paid**
7. Overdue unpaid invoices show **Overdue** as text, not colour alone

## Search

Use the Search button or Ctrl+F to find customers, catalogue items, quotations, and invoices.

## Reports

Reports cover sales (today / week / month / year / custom), outstanding invoices, customer sales, and quotation conversion rate.

## If something goes wrong

Destructive actions ask for confirmation. Restoring a backup always creates a safety copy first. Activity is listed at the bottom of Settings.
